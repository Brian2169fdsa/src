Tommy's Story

01

16 Years of Substance Use Disorder

For over a decade and a half, Tommy was in the grip of a severe substance use disorder. He cycled through relapse, near-death, and the devastating weight of co-occurring mental health challenges that made every attempt at sobriety feel impossible.

02

Multiple Overdoses. Clinically Died.

Tommy overdosed multiple times. He was clinically dead on more than one occasion. What SRC knows to be true, that no case is too far gone, was proven in Tommy's survival against every statistic.

03

The Turning Point: Love as the Catalyst

It wasn't a court order alone that changed him. It was one moment of profound clarity: realizing his substance use disorder was destroying the person he loved most. That love became his anchor and his fuel.

04

Total Reinvention Through Faith, Discipline and Community

Tommy rebuilt his identity from zero through Christian faith, mentorship, and the Elliott Army. Today he earns $100k+ months, is happily married to Ciera, and is rooted in Arizona, proving that recovery is a launchpad, not a ceiling.

Why He's Speaking at SRC

Tommy's journey directly mirrors what Sanctuary Recovery Centers has championed for 20 years: that mental health and substance use disorder are connected, that healing is holistic, and that recovery opens the door to a life beyond what anyone imagined possible.

He Speaks to Co-Occurring Realities

Tommy's story weaves together substance use disorder and the emotional wounds beneath it, the integrated, whole-person perspective SRC delivers every day.

Living Proof of What SRC Believes

SRC's vision is to be a beacon of hope. Tommy is that hope made visible, a man who was clinically dead and now leads others to their greatest lives.

Recovery Beyond Sobriety

SRC's mission is True Healing and Continued Care. Not just abstinence, but a full and flourishing life. Tommy's seven-figure transformation proves that story to every person in the room.

A Faith-Rooted, Whole-Person Message

SRC treats the mind, body, and spirit. Tommy speaks from the same truth: that lasting recovery requires rebuilding identity, not just removing substances.

20 Years of True Healing
and Continued Care™

For two decades, Sanctuary Recovery Centers (SRC) has been Arizona's most trusted destination for individuals navigating substance use disorder and co-occurring mental health conditions. From our Phoenix campus, we provide a full continuum of care, from mental health-centered treatment through sober living, virtual IOP, and long-term alumni support.

This anniversary isn't just a milestone. It's a celebration of every person who walked through our doors and chose to heal. Tommy Procopovich's story is that story, amplified. His presence at this event is a testament to what becomes possible when someone receives the right support, finds the right community, and refuses to give up.

Mental Health and Co-Occurring Care

SRC treats the whole person, addressing underlying mental health challenges alongside substance use disorder through evidence-based, individualized planning.

True Healing and Continued Care™

Recovery doesn't end at discharge. SRC walks alongside clients from inpatient treatment through sober living, virtual IOP, and lifelong alumni connection.

A Beacon of Hope for Arizona

SRC's vision is to transform addiction treatment, proving that sustainable recovery and a full, meaningful life are possible for everyone who walks through our doors.
